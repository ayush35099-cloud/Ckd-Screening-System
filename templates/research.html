1. Dataset Provenance & Preprocessing Pipeline

The model is trained on the authentic UCI Chronic Kidney Disease Dataset, originally contributed by Dr. P. Soundarapandian (Senior Consultant Nephrologist, Apollo Hospitals, Karaikudi, Tamil Nadu, India) and Dr. P. Eswaran (Alagappa University). The dataset comprises 400 patient records with 24 input laboratory and clinical variables (11 numerical, 13 categorical/ordinal) and 1 target classification column (250 CKD, 150 Not CKD).

Data Cleaning & Parsing

Resolved non-standard whitespace, tabs ('\t'), trailing commas, and standardized nominal labels (e.g. 'dm', 'cad', 'class').

Missing Value Handling

Quantitative metrics imputed with SimpleImputer(strategy='median'). Categorical variables imputed with SimpleImputer(strategy='most_frequent').

Data Leakage Prevention

Strict Pipeline encapsulation: preprocessing transforms are fit strictly on training folds and applied to test sets without leakage.

2. Comparative Evaluation: Decision Tree vs Random Forest

Because this is a medical screening application, the optimization objective prioritizes Sensitivity (Recall) to minimize False Negatives (cases where a CKD-positive patient is falsely labeled as healthy).

Academic Benchmark Evaluation Scope

The evaluation metrics presented below reflect retrospective testing on the 400-record UCI benchmark dataset (80-sample stratified held-out test split). While the Random Forest model achieved perfect sensitivity on this specific test partition, this reflects academic benchmark performance on standardized data and does not prove 100% real-world diagnostic certainty. Independent prospective clinical trials on diverse patient populations have not been performed.

Hematocrit percentage. Decreased renal synthesis of erythropoietin directly induces normocytic normochromic anemia.
