RenalCare Community Health Initiative

A machine learning decision-support platform designed to assist healthcare workers in Primary Health Centres (PHCs) and rural camps in detecting early physiological markers of Chronic Kidney Disease.

BCP308 Community Project • Team A-12
Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering, Bengaluru.

Training Dataset

UCI Chronic Kidney Disease Benchmark (400 cases, 24 laboratory attributes).

Educational & Screening Disclaimer: This application is an educational decision-support screening aid and is NOT a medical diagnostic device. It does not diagnose, cure, mitigate, or treat Chronic Kidney Disease. Any flagged indicator must be independently verified by a licensed medical practitioner through standard diagnostic nephrology protocols.
