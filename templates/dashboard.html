Multi-Dataset Model Comparison & Audit

Independent evaluation metrics across distinct clinical and population training distributions
2 Independent Datasets
Cohort & Population Distinction Notice:

The evaluation metrics below originate from independent training distributions and separate test cohorts. Model 1 was developed on the clinical retrospective UCI dataset (N=400, test N=80), featuring urinalysis dipsticks and a high baseline CKD prevalence (62.5%). Model 2 was developed on the official CDC NHANES 2017–2018 US continuous survey (N=5,063, test N=1,013), where derived CKD indicator prevalence is 18.55% under KDIGO criteria. These figures provide an independent audit of each pipeline on its native held-out distribution and should not be viewed as a direct head-to-head comparison on a shared patient population.
